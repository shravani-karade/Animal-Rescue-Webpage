Emergency Help & Guidance

Essential steps to take when finding an animal in distress.

1. Ensure Safety

Approach with caution. Keep a safe distance if the animal is startled, frightened, or aggressive.

2. Take Clear Photos

Capture clear pictures of the animal and recognizable landmarks to assist rescuers in locating them fast.

3. Submit a Report

Fill out the report form on RescuePaws so local volunteers receive real-time notifications.
